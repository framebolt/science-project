01Presentations — class mode

First, the slides then touch the waves

6 slides, about 3 minutes. Arrows, dots, or swipe. Full detail lives in steps ①–⑤ below.

Slide 1 / 6 — Signal

Signal = change that means something

A signal is a physical quantity that changes with time. When the change carries meaning, information travels from one point to another. Voice shakes air — that shaking pattern is the signal.

सिग्नल = समयसँग बदलिने, सूचना बोक्ने भौतिक परिमाण।

Slide 2 / 6 — Analog

Analog: smooth & continuous 〰

Analog: continuously changing physical quantity. Smooth sine wave, e.g., −5V to +5V. Natural voice, old radio. Peak = top, valley = bottom, amplitude = strength (supplementary terms).

लगातार परिवर्तन — जुनसुकै मान, smooth wave।

Slide 3 / 6 — Digital

Digital: fixed steps ▓

Only fixed levels — HIGH = 5V = 1, LOW = 0V = 0. Square-step waveform. This is what computers eat.

Fixed मान मात्र — HIGH = 1, LOW = 0।

Slide 4 / 6 — Binary

Binary: only 0 and 1

1 bit = one binary digit. 8 bits = 1 byte. Exam rule: n bits → 2ⁿ possible combinations. 3 bits → 8.

Bit: A bit is the smallest form of data on a computer. It is short form of binary digit and can be either 0 or 1. Byte: A group of eight bits which works as a single unit of data in computer.

1 bit = एउटा अंक, 8 bit = 1 byte, n bit → 2ⁿ।

Slide 5 / 6 — ADC · DAC

Bridges both ways

ADC: analog → digital (recording in). DAC: digital → analog (playing out). A→D in, D→A out.

रेकर्ड = ADC, बजाउने = DAC।

Slide 6 / 6 — On the wire

Send it, survive noise

Source → encode → channel → decode. Noise = unwanted signal added by the channel. Distortion = change in the signal's shape. Digital signals are protected from both by encoding and error-checking codes.

Full textbook chain: Source → Input Transducer → Encoder → Modulator → Channel → Demodulator → Decoder → Output Transducer → Output Signal.

Noise = फोहोर थपिनु; digital लाई सच्याउन मिल्छ।

04Fig.01 — What is a signal?

Signal = change that means something

A signal is a physical quantity that changes with time. When the change carries meaning, information travels from one point to another. Your voice shakes air. That shaking pattern is the signal. Same idea for voltage in a wire — or the brightness of light — change over time, carrying a message.
सिग्नल भनेको समयसँग परिवर्तन हुने भौतिक परिमाण हो जसले सूचना बोक्छ। बोलेको आवाजले हावा हल्लाउँछ — त्यही हल्लिने pattern नै सिग्नल हो। तारको भोल्टेज वा बत्तीको उज्यालो पनि त्यसैगरी सूचना बोक्छ।

05Why digital wins

Seven advantages of digital transmission

  1. Less deterioration and noise in digital signals.
  2. Circuits used for digital transmission are much more reliable.
  3. Cheaper and easier to design digital circuits than analog circuits.
  4. Less possibility of signal overlap and cross-talk.
  5. The properties of digital signals do not change in normal conditions.
  6. Encoding and compression maintain security of information.
  7. Error-finding and correcting codes mean less chance of error during transmission.
