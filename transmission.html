FIG.10 — THE WHOLE TRIP

Transmission

SOURCE→ ENCODE→ SEND→ CHANNEL→ RECEIVE→ DECODE → EAR
Phone, radio, TV, internet — same skeleton. Voice becomes signal, rides a channel (wire / air / fibre), gets rebuilt at the end. Noise = junk added on the way. Distortion = shape bent. Digital wins because it can be regenerated and error-checked.
टेलिफोन, रेडियो, टिभी, इन्टरनेट — खाका एउटै। आवाज सिग्नल बन्छ, channel (तार/हावा/फाइबर) मा यात्रा गर्छ, उतापट्टि फेरि बन्छ। Noise = बाटोमा थपिएको फोहोर, distortion = आकार बाङ्गिनु। Digital लाई regenerate + सच्याउन मिल्छ।
